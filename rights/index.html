Patients, Right?

Your rights in the hospital, and what to say to use them.

  1. 1

    Know It

    Understand your care before you agree to it.

    “So what I’m hearing is…”(then say it back)

  2. 2

    Own It

    See and get a copy of your records.

    Ask in writing, date it, keep a copy.

  3. 3

    Say No

    Refuse a test or treatment, and change your mind.

    “What happens if we wait?”

  4. 4

    Speak Up

    Help make your care plan.

    Write your top three questions before they walk in.

  5. 5

    Feel Safe

    Keep your information private.

    “Please take me out of the hospital directory.”

  6. 6

    Find Relief

    Have your pain checked and treated.

    “It stops me from ____. What’s the plan if this doesn’t help?”

  7. 7

    Demand Better

    Get safe care, and speak up when it isn’t.

    “May I speak with the charge nurse?”

  8. 8

    Call Backup

    Choose your visitors and get help with a complaint.

    “Who handles complaints here?”
