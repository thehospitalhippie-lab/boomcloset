      Have The Talk

      Most of us put it off until later, and later turns into an emergency room. Pick a calm day. Bring snacks. You don't need every answer in one sitting. Start with these.

      1. What matters most to you when things get hard?
      2. Who do you want speaking for you if you can't speak for yourself?
      3. Where do you want to be at the end, and who do you want there?
      4. What scares you about dying? What doesn't?
      5. What do you want to hear from us while you can still hear it?

      In Tennessee, two papers put The Talk in writing: an advance directive, where you name who speaks for you and what you want, and a POST form (Physician Orders for Scope of Treatment), which you fill out with your doctor when you're seriously ill.

      Tennessee's healthcare decisions page (opens in a new tab) has the free forms.
