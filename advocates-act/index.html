First, put your own oxygen mask on.

When you're scared or furious about someone you love, your body floods. Your heart races, your words scramble, and the thinking part of your brain goes quiet. That's biology, not weakness. It also makes it hard to argue for anybody.

Two minutes here buys you a clearer voice for the next hour. Nobody is going to fire you from caregiving for taking them.

Breathe with the big top

Six slow rounds: in for 4, out for 6. A longer out-breath tells your body the lion is back in its cage.

Feet on the floor

Press both feet down and name three things you can see. You're here, in this room.

Water, then words

A sip of water and one sentence written down: the single thing you need fixed most.

Call in a sidekick

Text one person to come or stay on the phone. They take notes; you do the talking.

Caregiver comfort, not medical advice. If the flood doesn't settle or you feel unsafe, reach out to your own doctor or call or text 988.

The lawsuit clock

Know your dates before you need them.

In Tennessee you generally have one year from an injury, or from when you found it, to start a health care lawsuit. Complaints and grievances don't pause it. Put in the date and see your rough timeline.

A rough guide from Tenn. Code Ann. § 29-26-116 and -121, not legal advice. Exceptions exist (minors, hidden errors, government hospitals). Let a Tennessee lawyer confirm your real dates.

Who to call in Tennessee

    
The referees.

    
They can investigate, cite and discipline. They can't award money, so pair them with a lawyer if someone was really hurt.

    

      
Health Facilities Commission
Hospitals, nursing homes, assisted living, home health and hospice.
File a complaint online or mail 502 Deaderick St., 9th Floor, Nashville 37243

      
Dept. of Health licensing boards
A specific doctor, nurse, pharmacist or therapist. You can file anonymously.
Report a concern · 1-800-852-2187

      
Acentra Health (Medicare)
Medicare patients: doctors review whether care met standards; fast appeals if discharged too soon.
Quality-of-care complaint · they'll help you write it

      
The Joint Commission
Safety concerns at accredited hospitals and agencies. Can be anonymous.
Online form, about two pages max

      
Long-Term Care Ombudsman
A free advocate for residents of nursing homes and assisted living.
877-236-0013 routes you to your district

      
HHS Office for Civil Rights
Privacy breaches, records requests ignored, or discrimination.
hhs.gov/ocr, usually within 180 days
