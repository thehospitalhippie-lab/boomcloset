WHO WE ARE

Your hosts are hospice nurses

Golden Soul Circus was founded by hospice nurses who have been at the bedside for hundreds of deaths — the beautiful ones and the devastating ones.

Brad Burns

Hospice nurse · Co-founder · Licensed 2016

Years of med/surg, ICU, float pool, COVID, and wound care before finding his way to hospice. Raised on singing telegrams and trained in Shakespearean stage acting, Brad brings improv, music, and movement into the hardest conversations — so families can find a little relief and smile awhile.

Cass Burns

Hospice nurse · Co-founder · Licensed 2017

Cass has most recently served patients of earth in end-of-life (EOL) care. After a journey through all categories of ICU nursing, including burn, trauma, covid, and a range of medical and surgical units, Cass left the critical care setting for more holistic and intimate care in integrative wellness and home hospice nursing. In more than 10 years of patient and community care, first as a care partner and now in her ninth year as a nurse, Cass has witnessed the full spectrum of quality lives and deaths. She is honored to share those findings in a welcoming and joyful manner to open minds and hearts around health and wellness, life and death.

"We're all nurses — just some of us are registered."
